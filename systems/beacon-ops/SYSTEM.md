# Beacon Ops

> Original Operations system. Binding values live in `tokens.css`.

## Product truth

Beacon Ops makes evidence feel tangible: a #111612 ground, #19231B/#233027 elevation sequence, #F1F7EE primary ink, #AABBA8 supporting ink and one #8FCE78 signal accent. The display face is "Source Serif 4"; text uses Inter Tight and data uses JetBrains Mono.

## Composition contract

The three licensed directions are `editorial-ledger`, `split-proof`, `rail-story`. Each page still needs four section shapes, two widths, one inspectable artifact and no more than two signature moments.

## Material and motion

Light enters above-left. Use the three declared elevation levels, grain on fills above 40vh, a transform-only arrival and an immediate reduced-motion state. Radius nesting is 6px → 12px → 20px.
