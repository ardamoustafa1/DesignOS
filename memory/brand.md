# Brand

- Voice: decisive, evidence-led, technically exact, and intolerant of unearned quality claims.
- Product belief: taste must be made operational through constraints, examples, and proof—not remembered as aesthetic trivia.
- Visual direction: calm system chrome with scarce, product-truth signature moments.
- Motion language: context-selected; interaction feedback is fast, interruptible, compositor-safe, and paired with a reduced-motion policy.
- Naming rule: capability names describe the decision or evidence they provide (`browser-gate`, `motion-decide`, `coverage`).
